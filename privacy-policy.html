GCR Graphix — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how GCR Graphix (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our poster generation, admin portal, user portal, WhatsApp messaging, and Facebook Page posting services (the “Service”).

By using the Service, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Who we are

GCR Graphix provides tools for businesses and campaign teams to create promotional posters and share them with registered users via WhatsApp and/or Facebook.

Contact: support@gcrgraphix.com

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Service, we may collect:

  • Account and profile data: name, mobile number, address, city, state, district, pincode, occupation type, shop type, category, gender, and related registration fields provided by you or your administrator.
  • Images: profile photos, poster images, reference photos, and shared media uploaded for poster creation or distribution.
  • WhatsApp-related data: mobile number, message delivery status, and timestamps of inbound WhatsApp messages (used to determine whether we can send media directly within Meta’s messaging window).
  • Facebook-related data: when you connect Facebook, we receive information about Facebook Pages you manage (Page ID, Page name, and Page access tokens) so we can post posters to the Page you select. We may also store your Facebook user ID linked to your app account.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, and logs needed to operate and secure the Service.
  • Admin access data: administrator usernames and authentication activity for the internal admin portal.

3. How we use information

We use collected information to:

  • Create, customize, and deliver posters and promotional images
  • Send images and messages to users on WhatsApp when requested by an authorized admin
  • Post images to a Facebook Page you connect and authorize
  • Manage user accounts and Facebook connections
  • Improve reliability, security, and support
  • Comply with applicable law and platform requirements (Meta, Twilio, etc.)

4. Legal basis (where applicable)

We process personal data based on your consent, performance of the Service you request, legitimate interests in operating and securing the platform, and compliance with legal obligations.

5. Third-party services

We use trusted third parties to run the Service, including:

  • MongoDB — database hosting
  • Cloudinary — image storage and delivery
  • Twilio — WhatsApp messaging
  • Meta / Facebook — Facebook Login and Page posting
  • Render — application hosting
  • AI providers (e.g. fal.ai) — optional image generation when enabled

These providers process data according to their own privacy policies and our instructions, only as needed to provide the Service.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data:

  • With service providers listed above
  • With Meta or Twilio to deliver Facebook or WhatsApp features you enable
  • When required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety
  • With your administrator or team members authorized to manage your account

7. Data retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the Service, maintain Facebook/WhatsApp integrations, resolve disputes, and meet legal requirements. Facebook connection tokens are retained while your connection is active and may expire according to Meta’s policies.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, including HTTPS, access controls for admin accounts, and secure storage with our hosting and cloud providers. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal information
  • Withdraw consent where processing is consent-based
  • Disconnect Facebook from your account
  • Request that we stop sending WhatsApp messages to your number

To make a request, contact us at the email above. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

10. Facebook data

If you use Facebook Login, we access only the permissions you approve (such as listing Pages you manage and posting to a Page you select). You can remove our access at any time in your Facebook settings under Settings & privacy → Settings → Security and login → Apps and websites, or by asking us to disconnect your account.

For data deletion requests related to Facebook-connected data, email us with your registered mobile number or user ID. We will delete or anonymize associated Facebook connection data within a reasonable time, subject to legal retention requirements.

11. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. International transfers

Your data may be processed in countries where our providers operate. We take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards where required.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the updated policy.

14. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data: support@gcrgraphix.com
